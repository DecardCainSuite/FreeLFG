# Free LFG — changelog

Newest first. Each release's notes come from `CHANGELOG.md` at the time it was published.

## Free LFG 1.0.20

- Group tooltip: the members no longer disappear when the game does not answer again on hover. Free LFG keeps the members it read with the search results, and shows any member still unknown as "member" under its role, next to the open slots.
- The tooltip redraws itself when the game sends new details while it is open.
- New `/flfg members` command: lists the member data the game left incomplete (helps with bug reports).

## Free LFG 1.0.19

- "Searching..." no longer stays in the footer once the results are in.
- Changing the category clears the results of the previous one until you press Refresh.
- The Custom message preview no longer shows a `/w` command (names with a space are always handled by the game's whisper box).

## Free LFG 1.0.18

- First release: the group finder as a compact, sortable table, live filters, one-click whisper from a template, **Custom** message start, invite for solo players, detailed tooltip with members and roles.
